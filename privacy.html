Privacy Policy

Boda Dispatch · Last updated: 21 November 2026

Boda Dispatch is a delivery request service that customers use through WhatsApp. This page explains what information we collect, why, and how you can have it removed.

Information we collect

  • Your WhatsApp phone number and, if available, your WhatsApp profile name.
  • The messages you send to our WhatsApp number and the replies we send you.
  • Delivery details you provide, such as pickup and drop-off locations, recipient name and phone number, package description and urgency.

How we use it

We use this information only to create, assign and complete your delivery requests, and to contact you or the recipient about a delivery.

Who can see it

Your information is visible only to our dispatch team and the rider assigned to your delivery. We do not sell or share your information with third parties for marketing. Messages are delivered through the WhatsApp Business Platform operated by Meta, which processes them under its own terms.

How long we keep it

We keep delivery records and message history for as long as needed to provide the service and handle any questions about past deliveries.

Deleting your data

To have your information deleted, email shanilamalesa01@icloud.com with the phone number you used, and we will remove it.
